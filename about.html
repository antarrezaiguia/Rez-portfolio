About Me

I'm an HSE Engineer with nearly seven years of experience in the Oil & Gas industry in Algeria, with a strong focus on firefighting, emergency response, industrial risk management, and process safety. I've been directly involved in real accident and incident response, making on-site decisions under pressure, as well as in emergency simulation exercises (including PPI drills) where I helped organize and assign roles to firefighting teams. Working at remote Oil & Gas sites in the Sahara has also taught me to respond responsibly when conventional emergency services are far away.

My academic background includes two Master's degrees from two different universities: a Master's Degree in Industrial Risk Management from the University of Batna and a Master's in QHSE from the University of Ouargla, along with a Bachelor's degree in Technical English from the University of Tébessa. Alongside my field experience, I've been self-teaching risk modeling tools such as PHAST, and building skills in Excel and Power BI — turning large, dense reports into clear dashboards to support faster and better-informed safety decisions. I'm now also developing Python skills to expand this data-driven approach further.

Today, I'm building a professional profile at the intersection of Oil & Gas HSE, industrial risk, emergency response, and data-driven decision-making. My languages are Arabic (mother tongue), French, English, and Spanish (A1). I'm open to international opportunities and to work where field experience, technical analysis, and data can come together.
