HSE Expertise

My HSE expertise has been developed through nearly seven years of experience in the Oil & Gas industry, particularly in operational and remote-field environments where emergency preparedness, risk control, and practical decision-making are critical.

Firefighting & Emergency Response

Practical knowledge of firefighting principles, fire prevention, and response to incidents in challenging and isolated field environments — including maintenance and exploitation of firefighting equipment and pumps, and weekly preparation of emergency exercises and drills.

Fire & Explosion Risk Assessment

Assessment of fire and explosion scenarios, including hazardous phenomena, escalation mechanisms, and their implications for people, assets, and operations.

Fire & Explosion Modelling

Engineering calculations and simulation of fire and explosion scenarios using specialized software, including thermal radiation, overpressure, dispersion, and hazardous impact zone assessment.

Industrial & Process Safety Risk Management

Identification and assessment of industrial hazards within Oil & Gas operations, consequence analysis, risk evaluation, safety barriers, and practical measures to reduce risk.

Incident & Emergency Management

Experience handling emergency situations in remote, isolated environments where distance limits access to external emergency resources — including preparing full reports on firefighting safety during real interventions.

First Aid & Team Training

Occasional first aid training delivered to colleagues, combining practical field knowledge with a hands-on teaching approach.

HSE Data & Analytics

Developing capabilities in Excel, Power BI, and Python to analyze HSE data, build dashboards and insights, identify trends, and support data-driven risk management and decision-making.

HSE Training & Continuous Development

Continuous self-development through specialized training in firefighting, emergency response, industrial safety, and other HSE-related disciplines.
